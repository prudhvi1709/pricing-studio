Scenario Engine - 3 Elasticity Models

Simulate pricing scenarios across acquisition, churn, and tier migration models

3-Model Framework: Each tab represents a distinct elasticity model with different customer responses and decision metrics.

Model 1: Acquisition Elasticity (Gross Adds)

What it captures: How gross adds change across candidate price points and promos

Key Insight: High-intent joiners remain stable across P+Δ. Price-sensitive joiners show accelerated adds loss beyond P+Δ.

Decision Pack Outputs: Gross Adds, Revenue Impact, Payback Horizon, Promo Required

Model 2: Churn Elasticity (Lagged/Horizon-Based)

What it captures: When churn shows up after price changes or promo roll-offs (time-lag effect)

Key Insight: Churn impact arrives weeks after the event. Roll-off concentrates churn after discount ends. Peak typically at 8-12 weeks.

Decision Takeaway: Choose the narrowest gap that increases Ad-Free share while keeping downgrade + cancel within guardrails.
